## Speeds and costs
Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days and is free on orders over $75; below that it costs $6.95.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $19.95.

## Where we ship
Orders ship from Denver, Colorado to the United States and Canada.

## Address changes
A shipping address can be changed until the order status is shipped.
