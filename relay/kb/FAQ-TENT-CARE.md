---
title: FAQ: tent care
acl: public
---

Dry your tent fully before storing it to prevent mildew. Clean it with lukewarm water and a non-detergent soap; never machine wash.
Re-seal seams every two seasons with a seam sealer. Small fly tears can be patched with the included repair kit;
larger tears or broken poles on a tent under 2 years old are covered by the warranty.
