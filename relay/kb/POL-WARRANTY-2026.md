## Coverage
Northwind gear carries a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects such as seam failures, broken poles,
faulty zippers, and delamination. Normal wear, misuse, and accidental damage are not covered.

## How to claim
Submit photos and the order number through the warranty form at northwind.example/warranty.
We repair, replace, or issue store credit, usually within 10 business days.
Warranty claims do not depend on the return window.
