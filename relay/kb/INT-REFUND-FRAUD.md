---
title: Internal: refund fraud signals
acl: group:support
---

Escalate to the fraud team before approving any refund when two or more of these apply: three or more refunds in 90 days,
a new account under 14 days old, a shipping address that differs from billing on a high-value order, or a request to refund to a different card.
Never tell the customer which signals triggered the review.
