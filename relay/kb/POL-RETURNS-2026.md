## Return windows
Unused items in original condition can be returned within 60 days of delivery for a full refund.
Used tents and used sleeping bags can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Other used items follow the 60-day window.

## Refund timing and fees
Refunds go back to the original payment method and appear within 3 to 5 business days.
Return shipping is free for orders over $75; otherwise a $7.95 label fee is deducted from the refund.

## Exclusions
Items marked final sale, gift cards, and personalised items cannot be returned.
